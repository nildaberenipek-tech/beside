Before we start

At night, Lambaura watches for the strong movements of a seizure and gets the people you choose to check on you. For that it needs a few permissions. You can turn them off later in your phone settings.

Motion
To notice the rhythmic jerking of a convulsive seizure while you sleep.
Location
Only sent in an emergency, so your people know where to come.
Microphone
Only when you turn on listening for your code word. Nothing is recorded.
Your log
Kept on this phone. It only leaves when you share a report.
Lambaura is a research prototype, not a medical device.
It can miss seizures and it can raise false alarms. Keep following your doctor's advice.

Timing

Aura

Say it when you feel an aura. It tells your people and starts a check-in. It never calls an ambulance on its own.

Use it with the phone locked (Siri)
  1. Open the Shortcuts app, then tap + to make a new shortcut.
  2. Add Get Contents of URL. URL: . Method: POST. Body: text "Aura reported".
  3. Add Send Message to your contact with the text "I feel an aura, please check on me".
  4. Name the shortcut with your code word, then try it: lock the phone and say "Hey Siri, your code word".
Use it with the phone locked (Samsung and Android)
  1. Samsung: open Bixby Routines or Modes and Routines and make a routine started by a voice command.
  2. Other Android phones: in the Google Assistant app, create a routine with your code word.
  3. Make the routine send a message to your contact.

Emergency buttons on your phone

iPhone: Settings → Emergency SOS. Pressing the side button 5 times calls emergency services and texts your emergency contacts.

Samsung: Settings → Safety and emergency → Emergency SOS. Pressing the side key several times sends your location to your contacts.
