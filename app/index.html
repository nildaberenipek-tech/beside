Beside

Before we start

Beside watches over you at night and asks the people you choose to check on you. To do that it needs a few permissions. You can change your mind at any time in your phone settings.

Movement sensor
To notice the strong, rhythmic movements of a convulsive seizure while you sleep.
Location
Only added to an emergency message, so your contacts know where to come.
Microphone
Only while aura listening is on, to hear your code word. Audio is not recorded or sent.
Your health log
Saved only on this phone. It leaves the phone only when you choose to share a report.
Beside is a research prototype, not a medical device.
It can miss seizures and it can raise false alarms. Keep following your doctor's safety advice.

Your doctor

Your doctor gets a report only when you share it. Emergency messages go to the people below, not to your doctor, because a doctor may not see a message at night.

Aura

Say it when you feel an aura. It turns on high-attention mode; it never calls an ambulance by itself.

People to contact

Beside writes the message and opens it for you; you tap Send. A website is not allowed to send messages on its own.

Comfort and light

Beside never flashes or flickers. To soften flashing in other apps too, your iPhone has a built-in setting:

Settings → Accessibility → Motion → Dim Flashing Lights

Ask your doctor whether light sensitivity matters for your type of epilepsy.
